## Full description（上限 4000 字符）

```
What's really in your chats?

Chat Report turns an exported chat history into a real report about your relationship — not a horoscope, but numbers you can trace back to actual messages.

WHAT YOU GET

• Warmth score — one number, 0 to 100, for how the relationship reads right now
• Balance — who sends more, who starts conversations, and how fast each of you replies
• Emotion curve — a month-by-month line that marks your warmest month and your rough patch
• Topics — what the two of you actually talk about, ranked
• Chat personalities — three playful tags for each of you, drawn from real habits
• The full reading — a long-form take on your rhythm, your turning points, and what each of you seems to care about, with quotes and dates

BUILT ON REAL NUMBERS

Every statistic — message counts, reply times, who texts first — is calculated on your phone from your own export. The AI is used to interpret those numbers and your conversations, never to invent them. Quotes come with dates so you can check them yourself.

PRIVACY FIRST

• Your chat file is parsed and measured entirely on your device
• To write your report, the chat text is sent to our AI provider over an encrypted connection — you're asked first, and we never store it
• Nothing is stored on our servers, and there is no account to create
• When you share a report card, names are automatically shortened to initials — there is no way to turn that off

HOW TO USE IT

1. Open your messaging app and export a conversation as a .txt file (text only, no media)
2. Open Chat Report and pick the file — the app walks you through it
3. Choose who you are and what the relationship is
4. Get your report in about 30 seconds

In a hurry? Paste any conversation straight into the app for a free quick read.

Not sure yet? Open the sample report in the app to see exactly what you'll get — no export needed.

WHO IT'S FOR

Couples curious about their own rhythm. People trying to read a situationship. Best friends who want a year-in-review of their inside jokes. Anyone looking back at a chapter that ended.

A NOTE ON WHAT THIS IS NOT

Chat Report describes patterns in a conversation. It is not therapy, not a diagnosis, and not a verdict on anybody. Treat it as a mirror worth talking about, not a scoreboard.

Chat Report is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any messaging service.
```
> ~2,200 字符，留有余量。最后一行免责声明**必须保留** —— 避免与消息服务方产生关联暗示。
